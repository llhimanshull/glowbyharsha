About Us

Glow by Harsha was founded by Harsha, a beauty professional with over 8 years of hands-on experience in the salon and wellness industry. Harsha started her journey with a passion for helping people look and feel their best. Over the years, she has mastered a wide range of services including skincare, hair care, and grooming, while always keeping client comfort and satisfaction as her top priority. With her dedication and personal touch, Harsha has built Glow by Harsha into a trusted place where clients know they will receive genuine care, quality service, and honest advice. Her vision is to make beauty care accessible, comfortable, and confidence-boosting for everyone who walks into the studio.
